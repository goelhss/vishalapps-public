# CarMediaPlayer Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 21, 2026

CarMediaPlayer is a music and podcast player provided by Vishal Goel. It plays audio that you choose from your device and podcast feeds that you choose to add. This policy applies to CarMediaPlayer (`com.vishalgoel.carmediaplayer`), including its phone and Android Auto experiences.

## Information used by the app

- With your permission, the app reads music files and their metadata so it can display your library and play selected audio. When you choose a folder through Android's picker, access is limited to the location you select. Favorites, queues, playback history, podcast subscriptions and downloads, and saved lyrics are stored on your device.
- When you refresh a podcast feed, play or download an episode, or load its artwork, your device makes a request to the feed provider or other host of that content. The host may receive ordinary connection information, such as your IP address, and the URL requested.
- If you choose to search for lyrics, the app sends the song title and, when available, artist information to LRCLIB (`lrclib.net`) to return matching lyrics. The requested lyrics may be saved locally on your device. You can delete cached lyrics in the app.
- CarMediaPlayer does not ask you to create a CarMediaPlayer account. It does not include PersonalConsole's voice recording, library-card, calendar, stock, diagnostic-upload, or Google Drive backup features. Diagnostic data is not uploaded automatically; it leaves your device only if you explicitly share it.

## Advertising and third-party services

The current production release does not show ads or load a production advertising SDK. Development builds may show test ad banners on phone screens. A future release may introduce consent-aware phone advertising, in which case this policy and the Play Data safety declaration must be updated before that release. Ads are not shown in Android Auto, playback notifications, or background playback.

Podcast providers and LRCLIB handle requests under their own privacy practices. Android, Google Play, and Google Assistant may process information as part of the operating system or services you choose to use; their privacy practices are governed by their own policies. CarMediaPlayer does not sell your personal information.

## Your choices and deletion

You can decline or revoke audio and folder access in Android settings. You can remove podcast subscriptions, downloads, saved lyrics, and other local data in the app where controls are provided. Clearing the app's storage or uninstalling it removes its locally stored data. Requests already sent to a podcast provider or LRCLIB are subject to that provider's retention practices.
